## Verify before launch

1. **Leadership page.** It shows *Tan Chong Huat – Chairman* and *Victor Tay – Chief Executive Officer*. Both titles appear in public CABA-era sources (Mr Tan as CABA Chairman; "CEO Victor Tay" in CABA's 2021 year-end message). Confirm they are current for APBA, add photos, and add or remove people as the live site shows.
2. **Type of Membership options** (Register Your Interest form) are set to "Corporate Membership" and "Individual Membership". Match them to the live form under *Contact → Contact Forms → Register Your Interest*.
3. **Country list.** It covers ASEAN members, major partner markets and "Other". Extend it if the live form uses a full country list.
4. **Phone number.** None was found in public sources, so the field is empty and hidden. Add one in the Customizer if the live site lists one.
5. **Privacy Policy.** This is a PDPA-based template written for this build. Replace it with APBA's published policy or have it reviewed.
6. **Images.** Logo, hero, About, Summit and portrait images are neutral placeholders. Replace them with the live site's assets, keeping the same aspect ratios (hero ~2.3:1, cards 16:9, portraits 4:5).
7. **Brand colours and fonts.** See `DESIGN-INVENTORY.md`. Sample them from the live logo and stylesheet, then update Elementor Site Settings.
8. **Secretariat page.** The list of things the Secretariat handles summarises the published services (membership, missions/events, consultancy/courses, general enquiries). Adjust it to the live wording.
9. **Footer membership text** (Customizer → APBA Site Options → Footer) paraphrases the published membership benefits.
